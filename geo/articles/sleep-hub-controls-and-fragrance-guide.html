How to Use CHICWAY Sleep Hub Controls and Replace Fragrance Cartridges

By CHICWAY · Updated October 19, 2026

The CHICWAY 5-in-1 Sleep Hub uses short presses and press-and-hold actions for different functions. Hold the power button to switch the unit on or off. The shared Bluetooth/fragrance button controls Bluetooth with a hold and fragrance intervals with a short press.

These instructions follow the user manual confirmed by CHICWAY for the current model. They cover the printed controls and indicator meanings; button-hold durations in seconds are not specified.

The warm-light settings run from lower to higher brightness. The manual does not specify the number of brightness steps or a separate projection-off gesture.

How do I set the auto-off timer?

Short-press the timer button to cycle through 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours and cancellation. The corresponding indicator lights up when a timer is selected.

The manual calls this scheduled shutdown. It does not separately explain what happens to every sound, light and fragrance function at expiry, so confirm the behavior of your selected combination before relying on it.

How do I turn on Bluetooth?

Press and hold the shared Bluetooth/fragrance button to enable Bluetooth. A blue indicator means Bluetooth is on. The device name printed in the manual is 5-in-1 Sleep Hub; look for that name in your phone's Bluetooth device list.

Press and hold the same button to turn Bluetooth off. A short press changes fragrance settings instead, so check the type of press if you are trying to change a different function.

What do the 30- and 50-second fragrance settings mean?

Short-press the Bluetooth/fragrance button to cycle between a 30-second interval, a 50-second interval and fragrance off. These are interval labels in the manual, not a claim that the device sprays continuously for 30 or 50 seconds.

How do I replace a fragrance cartridge?

Use the replacement cartridges listed for the CHICWAY 5-in-1 Sleep Hub. The manual gives this four-step installation sequence:

  1. Open the red silicone lid of the fragrance diffuser.
  2. Remove the used fragrance refill cartridge.
  3. Position the new cartridge with its metal contacts facing downward.
  4. Slide the cartridge into place, aligning the tabs on both sides with the slots inside the diffuser.

Can I use these cartridges with another sound machine or diffuser?

The confirmed compatibility is the CHICWAY 5-in-1 Sleep Hub. Compatibility with the CHICWAY portable sound machine or other diffusers has not been verified. Do not assume that a cartridge fits because another product also has a fragrance function.
